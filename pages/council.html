COUNCIL & LEADERSHIP

Council & leadership.

The executive committee and council structures that lead Naledi Local Municipality.

STRUCTURE

How the council is structured.

The council is the municipality's highest decision-making body, chaired by the Speaker. The Executive Committee, chaired by the Mayor, provides political leadership and coordinates council business. Below the council are portfolio committees and the Municipal Public Accounts Committee (MPAC).

Council

Elected councillors responsible for policy and oversight.

Executive Committee

Coordinates and prioritises council programmes.

MPAC

Municipal Public Accounts Committee — financial oversight.

Portfolio committees

Focused oversight of service departments.

POWERS & FUNCTIONS

What the council does.

In terms of Section 4 of the Municipal Systems Act and Section 156 of the Constitution, the council exercises specific legislative and executive functions.

  1. Passes by-laws

    Adopts municipal by-laws for the good rule and government of the area.

  2. Approves budgets

    Adopts the annual budget and any adjustment budgets.

  3. Adopts the IDP

    Approves the Integrated Development Plan and reviews it annually.

  4. Oversees performance

    Monitors municipal performance against the SDBIP and annual reports.

  5. Appoints senior officials

    Appoints the Municipal Manager and executive directors.

  6. Holds the executive accountable

    Receives reports, asks questions, and takes resolutions on council matters.

KEY ROLES

Meet the office-bearers.

Council business is directed by the Speaker, the Mayor and the Chief Whip, each with distinct responsibilities under the Municipal Structures Act.

Office of the Mayor

Executive leadership, chairs the Executive Committee, political head of the municipality.

Office of the Speaker

Chairs council meetings, ensures rules of order, facilitates public participation.

Councillors

Elected representatives serving wards and proportional seats.
COUNCIL COMPOSITION

How councillors are elected.

Naledi Local Municipality is made up of elected representatives chosen through a mixed electoral system in terms of the Municipal Structures Act.

Ward councillors

Elected directly by voters in each ward to represent the ward's residents.

Proportional representation

Councillors elected from party lists, based on each party's share of the vote.

Ward committees

Community representatives elected per ward to advise their ward councillor.

COUNCIL COMMITTEES

Committees and their roles.

Executive Committee

Coordinates council business, prepares recommendations and monitors service delivery.

MPAC

Reviews audit reports, financial statements and the annual report. Recommends action to council.

Technical Services

Oversees water, sanitation, electricity, roads and infrastructure.

Community Services

Oversees waste management, parks, libraries, housing and social services.

Finance & Treasury

Oversees budgeting, revenue collection, expenditure control and financial reporting.

Corporate Services

Oversees human resources, ICT, legal services and council support.

Contact your councillor

Submit public comments

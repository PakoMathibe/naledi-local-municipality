WORKING WITH US

Build a career that matters.

Naledi Local Municipality offers a range of career opportunities across technical services, finance, community services, corporate services and planning. Vacancies are advertised on the official Naledi Vacancies page and in local media, and applications are submitted in line with the requirements of each advert.

Technical & engineering

Civil, electrical, water, sanitation and infrastructure roles.

Finance & revenue

Accountancy, SCM, billing, credit control and internal audit.

Community services

Waste, parks, libraries, social development and housing.

Corporate services

HR, ICT, legal, communications and council support.

Planning & development

IDP, town planning, local economic development and project management.

Public safety

Fire, disaster management, traffic and by-law enforcement.

APPLICATION PROCESS

Steps to apply.

  1. Read the advert

    Note the post, requirements, closing date and submission details.

  2. Prepare your documents

    Cover letter, CV, certified copies of qualifications and ID, and any other requested documents.

  3. Submit as instructed

    Follow the delivery method specified in the advert (hand delivery, post or email).

  4. Correspondence

    Only shortlisted candidates are contacted. If you have not been contacted by the stated date, consider your application unsuccessful.

STAY CONNECTED

How to keep informed.

Where do official notices appear?

Formal notices are published on the public notices page and in line with statutory requirements. Always check the official notice for comment deadlines and submission details.

How do I get the latest news?

Bookmark this page. You can also follow the municipality on Facebook, check the homepage news section, and call 053 928 2200 for general enquiries.

Where are tenders and vacancies advertised?

See the tenders and vacancies pages for the current listings and instructions.

Can I comment on a notice online?

This demo site does not collect submissions. On the production site, comment instructions are included in each notice — usually an email, a form, or a physical drop-off point.

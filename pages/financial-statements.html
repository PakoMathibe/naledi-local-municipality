FINANCE & REPORTS

Financial statements.

Audited annual financial statements, GRAP accounting and the Auditor-General's outcomes.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

What the statements contain.

The municipality prepares annual financial statements in line with the Municipal Finance Management Act (MFMA) and Generally Recognised Accounting Practice (GRAP). These statements are audited by the Auditor-General of South Africa and published with the annual report.

Statement of financial position

Assets, liabilities and net assets at year-end.

Statement of financial performance

Revenue, expenditure and surplus or deficit for the year.

Notes to the statements

Detailed disclosure of accounting policies, commitments and contingencies.

Audit report

The Auditor-General's opinion and findings on the statements.

UNDERSTANDING THE AUDIT

What the audit opinion means.

Unqualified (clean)

The Auditor-General is satisfied that the statements fairly represent the financial position.

Qualified

There are specific issues where the Auditor-General disagrees with certain figures or disclosures.

Adverse or disclaimer

Significant concerns about the reliability of the statements, or insufficient evidence to form an opinion.

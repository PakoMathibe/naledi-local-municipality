VISIT NALEDI

Tourism in Naledi.

Discover the attractions, heritage and hospitality of Vryburg, Stella, Dithakwaneng and Devondale.

EXPLORE OUR REGION

Welcome to the North West.

Naledi sits at the heart of the western North West, a region known for its agricultural heritage, open landscapes and welcoming communities. Whether you are visiting on business or passing through on the N14, there is plenty to discover.

Heritage

Historic sites and local heritage tied to the region's agricultural and mining roots.

Nature

Open landscapes and reserves in and around the district.

Local hospitality

Guest houses, restaurants and local produce.

Getting around

Road and rail access to the wider North West region.

ATTRACTIONS

What to see.

From the historic town centre of Vryburg to the open farming landscape around Stella, the Naledi area offers a mix of heritage, nature and small-town hospitality.

Vryburg town centre

Historic buildings and the atmosphere of a classic North West agricultural town — historically nicknamed "The Athens of the North West".

Farming countryside

Cattle, game, maize and groundnut farms across the district — a chance to see working agricultural land.

Open landscapes

Wide open spaces and quiet roads that characterise the western North West.

Local hospitality

Guest houses, restaurants and farm stalls serving the region.

Photography

Sunset over the flat plains, farm gates, old buildings — great photographic country.

Local produce

Seasonal farm produce and small-town retail along the N14 corridor.

PLAN YOUR VISIT

Visitor tips.

  1. Check seasonal conditions

    Summer storms are common — plan for the weather.

  2. Book accommodation early

    Vryburg is a convenient stop on the N14 and fills up during peak periods.

  3. Ask the municipality

    Contact us for referrals to local guest houses, restaurants and events.

  4. Respect local spaces

    Help keep shared and heritage sites clean and safe.

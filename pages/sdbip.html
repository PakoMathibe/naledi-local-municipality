PLANNING & PERFORMANCE

Service Delivery and Budget Implementation Plan.

Translating the IDP and budget into measurable service delivery targets for the year.

WHAT IS THE SDBIP?

The annual implementation plan.

The SDBIP is the annual implementation plan that links the IDP and budget to measurable service delivery targets. It sets out the KPIs and targets for each department and forms the basis for in-year and annual performance reporting.

KPIs & targets

Measurable indicators for each priority.

Quarterly milestones

Targets broken down per quarter for monitoring.

Reporting

Basis for mid-year and annual performance reports.

Budget link

Aligns each target to the approved budget.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

Structure of the SDBIP.

Departmental scorecards

Each department has its own scorecard of KPIs and quarterly targets.

Top-layer KPIs

A small set of council-level indicators summarising the year's priorities.

Budget alignment

Each target is linked to the budget line that funds it.

Quarterly projections

Targets are broken down into Q1, Q2, Q3 and Q4 for tracking.

Ward-level detail

Where applicable, targets are allocated per ward for equitable service delivery.

Performance agreements

Senior managers' performance agreements derive from the SDBIP.

MONITORING

How the SDBIP is tracked.

  1. Approval (Jul)

    The SDBIP is approved by the Mayor within 28 days of the budget.

  2. In-year monitoring (Aug – Jun)

    Monthly and quarterly reports track performance against targets.

  3. Mid-year review (Jan)

    Progress and any necessary adjustments are reviewed mid-year.

  4. Annual reporting (Aug)

    Year-end performance is reported in the annual report.

ROLE

Who the Municipal Manager is.

The Municipal Manager heads the municipal administration and is the Accounting Officer in terms of the Municipal Finance Management Act (MFMA). The office translates council decisions into implementation, manages the executive directors and ensures the municipality delivers its IDP and budget commitments lawfully, efficiently and accountably.

The role is defined by Section 54A and Section 55 of the Municipal Systems Act, and Section 60 of the MFMA. The Municipal Manager is accountable to council and, through council, to the community of Naledi.

REPORTING TO COUNCIL

Statutory reporting obligations.

The Municipal Manager reports to council on a defined schedule, in line with the MFMA and the Municipal Systems Act.

  1. SDBIP & top-layer KPIs

    Submitted to the Mayor within 14 days of the budget being approved, and to council within 28 days.

  2. Monthly budget statements

    Submitted to the Mayor and the provincial treasury within 10 working days of the end of each month.

  3. Mid-year budget & performance report

    Submitted by 25 January each year, with recommendations for adjustment.

  4. Quarterly performance reports

    Submitted to council each quarter, tracking SDBIP delivery.

  5. Annual report & financial statements

    Submitted to council and the Auditor-General within statutory deadlines.

  6. Annual performance report

    Reported in the annual report and considered by MPAC and council.

SERVICE PRINCIPLES

Guided by Batho Pele. People first.

The Office of the Municipal Manager is committed to the Batho Pele principles — consultation, service standards, access, courtesy, information, openness & transparency, redress, and value for money. These principles guide how every department serves the community of Naledi.

If you believe a service standard was not met, you can raise a concern through the contact page or by calling 053 928 2200.

MEETINGS & PROCEEDINGS

Council meetings.

Schedules, agendas, minutes and public attendance information for council, Executive Committee and portfolio committee meetings.

OPEN, TRANSPARENT COUNCIL

Decisions made in public.

Council meetings are open to the public, except where council resolves to consider confidential matters in terms of Section 20 of the Municipal Systems Act. Agendas and minutes are published so residents can follow decisions and hold the municipality accountable.

Schedule

Council, Executive Committee and portfolio committee meeting calendar.

Agendas

Published at least 7 days before each meeting for public reading.

Minutes

Confirmed records of decisions and council resolutions.

Public attendance

Members of the public may attend open sessions.

TYPES OF MEETINGS

Who meets and why.

Ordinary Council

Quarterly meetings of full council to consider policy, budgets, reports and council resolutions. Chaired by the Speaker.

Executive Committee (EXCO)

Monthly meetings chaired by the Mayor to coordinate council programmes and recommend to council.

MPAC

Municipal Public Accounts Committee, meeting to review finances, the audit report and performance.

Portfolio Committees

Focused oversight committees covering technical services, community services, finance and corporate services.

Special Council

Called by the Speaker when urgent matters require council decision outside the ordinary schedule.

Ward Committee Meetings

Ward-level engagement where residents and ward councillors discuss local issues and priorities.

BEFORE ATTENDING

What to expect.

  1. Check the schedule

    Confirm meeting dates, times and venues via public notices or by calling 053 928 2200.

  2. Read the agenda

    Agendas are published at least 7 days before each meeting, available from the municipality.

  3. Check the venue

    Most council meetings are held in the Council Chamber at 19A Market Street, Vryburg.

  4. Understand the rules

    Follow the Speaker's rules and the code of conduct. Public attendance is a right, but public participation is at the Speaker's discretion.

  5. Request minutes

    Confirmed minutes are published after each meeting. Contact the office for older copies.

PUBLIC PARTICIPATION

Your right to attend.

Members of the public have the right to attend open council meetings. Speaking rights are subject to the Speaker's rules. If you wish to address council, you should request permission in advance from the Office of the Speaker.

Attend as an observer

Open meetings may be observed by any member of the public. No registration is required for observation.

Request speaking rights

Requests to address council must be submitted to the Office of the Speaker in advance.

Confidential sessions

Council may resolve to consider certain matters in closed session as permitted by law.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Common water & sanitation questions.

How do I report a water leak?

Call 053 928 2200 during office hours or the rapid response line on 053 928 2252. Provide the street address, nearest intersection and a brief description of the leak.

Why is my water discoloured or cloudy?

Air bubbles or sediment after maintenance can briefly cloud water. Run a cold tap for a few minutes. If the problem persists, report it to the municipality so the line can be inspected.

Who is responsible for the pipe on my property?

Generally, the municipality is responsible up to the water meter; the property owner is responsible for piping on the property side of the meter. Contact us to confirm for your specific property.

How do I query a water bill?

Bring your latest municipal account and meter number to the Revenue office at 19A Market Street, Vryburg, or call 053 928 2200.

What if I have no water at all?

Check whether neighbours are affected. If only your property, check your meter valve. Then call 053 928 2252 to report the outage.

What do I do if a sewer manhole is overflowing?

Treat it as an urgent fault. Keep people and pets away and call 053 928 2252 immediately.

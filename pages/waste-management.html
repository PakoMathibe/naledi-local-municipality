FREQUENTLY ASKED

Waste questions.

When is my refuse collected?

Collection days vary by area. Contact 053 928 2200 to confirm your collection day, or check official public notices for schedule changes.

How should I present my refuse for collection?

Place refuse in approved bags or bins outside your property on collection day. Ensure bins are accessible and not obstructed by vehicles.

Where do I report illegal dumping?

Report illegal dumping to 053 928 2200 with the location and a description of the material.

What can I recycle?

Common recyclables include clean paper, cardboard, glass, metal cans and plastic containers. Keep recyclables separate from general waste.

Can I put building rubble in my household bin?

No. Building rubble should be delivered to a designated municipal landfill or arranged through a private contractor.

How do I report a missed collection?

Call 053 928 2200 within 24 hours of your collection day and give your address. The service team will follow up.

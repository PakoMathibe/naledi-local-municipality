POLICY CATEGORIES

What the municipality has adopted.

The municipality adopts a range of policies and by-laws in line with legislation. Below are the main categories.

By-laws

Local rules adopted by council under the Municipal Systems Act — such as street trading, public conduct and refuse management.

Financial policies

Budget policy, credit control, supply chain management, asset management and banking policies.

Human resources

Employment equity, code of conduct, leave and performance-related policies.

Housing policies

Housing allocation, beneficiary administration and property management policies.

Service policies

Water, sanitation, electricity and waste service policies and standards.

Governance policies

Delegations, oversight, risk management and ethics policies.

HOW TO ACCESS

Request a policy.

Policy documents are available on request from the municipality. Contact 053 928 2200 or email info@naledigov.co.za and specify the policy you are looking for.

Draft policies under public comment are published on the public notices page.

ELECTED REPRESENTATIVES

Naledi councillors.

Elected representatives serving wards and proportional seats across the Naledi Local Municipality area.

YOUR REPRESENTATIVES

Who councillors are.

Councillors are elected to represent their communities, oversee municipal performance and take part in council decisions. Each councillor serves a five-year term and works with municipal officials to address local issues.

Ward councillors

Elected by voters in a specific ward to represent that ward's residents.

PR councillors

Proportional representation councillors elected from party lists.

Constituency work

Helping residents access services and resolve local issues.

Report-back meetings

Sharing information with communities about council matters.

ROLE OF A COUNCILLOR

What councillors do.

Councillors play a dual role — as elected representatives of their communities and as members of the municipal council. The Municipal Systems Act sets out their statutory responsibilities.

  1. Represent the community

    Bring community concerns and priorities into council discussions.

  2. Oversee the executive

    Hold the executive and administration accountable for service delivery.

  3. Attend council and committee meetings

    Participate in decision-making and voting on council matters.

  4. Engage with ward committees

    Work with elected ward committee members to prioritise local needs.

  5. Report back

    Inform communities about council decisions, budgets and services.

  6. Follow the code of conduct

    Act in the public interest, declare conflicts of interest and behave ethically.

FIND YOUR COUNCILLOR

How to reach yours.

If you're not sure who your ward councillor is, use one of these routes:

Call the switchboard

Phone 053 928 2200 and ask to be directed to your ward councillor.

Email the municipality

Email info@naledigov.co.za with your ward or address, and request the councillor's contact details.

Attend a ward meeting

Ward committee meetings and report-back sessions are the easiest way to meet your councillor in person.

WHEN TO CONTACT

The right channel.

Contact your councillor

For ward-level concerns, community feedback, or issues that need to be raised with council.

Contact the department

For service-specific queries — water, electricity, waste, accounts — the relevant department is faster.

Emergency services

For life-threatening emergencies, call 10111 (police), 10177 (ambulance) or the local rapid response line.

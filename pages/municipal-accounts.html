WHAT'S ON YOUR ACCOUNT

Charges on a municipal account.

Your municipal account is a combined statement covering several charges. Understanding each one helps you spot errors and query them quickly.

Property rates

Based on the municipal valuation of your property, as recorded in the valuation roll.

Water

Based on metered consumption, at the approved tariff per kilolitre.

Sanitation

Charged monthly based on your property category.

Refuse

Monthly charge for refuse removal in serviced areas.

PAYMENTS

Ways to pay.

At the municipal offices

Pay at 19A Market Street, Vryburg during office hours, Monday to Friday, 07:30–16:00.

Via your bank

Use your municipal account number as the reference when paying by EFT.

Authorised outlets

Certain retail outlets accept municipal account payments — confirm the outlet accepts Naledi accounts.

Bring your latest statement and ID when querying a payment, or contact the Revenue office on 053 928 2200.

INDIGENT SUPPORT

Help if you qualify.

If your household income is below the municipal threshold, you may qualify for indigent support — which can reduce or cover basic service charges. To apply, visit the Revenue office with proof of income and household details.

  1. Check eligibility

    Confirm your household income against the municipal threshold (contact the office for the current threshold).

  2. Collect the application form

    Available from the Revenue office or online (see forms page).

  3. Submit documents

    ID, proof of income, proof of residence and household details.

  4. Await assessment

    Applications are assessed by the Revenue department. You will be notified of the outcome.

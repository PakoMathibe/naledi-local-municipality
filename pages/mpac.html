ACCOUNTABILITY

Municipal Public Accounts Committee.

Council's committee for oversight of the municipality's finances and accountability.

WHAT MPAC DOES

Oversight that matters.

The Municipal Public Accounts Committee (MPAC) is a council committee that oversees the municipality's finances and performance, and reviews the Auditor-General's reports. MPAC makes recommendations to council to strengthen accountability and improve financial outcomes.

Financial oversight

Reviews financial statements, the audit report and related matters.

Performance

Reviews performance against the SDBIP and annual reports.

Recommendations

Makes recommendations to council on corrective action.

Public interest

Represents the community's interest in accountability.

WHAT MPAC REVIEWS

Documents and processes.

Annual report

Reviews the annual report and performance information before tabling in council.

Financial statements

Reviews the audited financial statements and Auditor-General's findings.

Audit action plan

Monitors the implementation of corrective actions arising from audit findings.

Performance reports

Reviews quarterly and annual performance reports against the SDBIP.

Oversight reports

Prepares oversight reports and recommendations for council decision.

Mid-year budget review

Reviews the mid-year budget and performance assessment.

HOW MPAC WORKS

Holding the municipality to account.

  1. Review reports

    The Auditor-General's report, annual reports and financial statements are reviewed.

  2. Engage departments

    MPAC meets with officials on specific findings and challenges.

  3. Recommend to council

    Recommendations are tabled at council for decision.

  4. Monitor progress

    Tracks corrective actions and follows up on outstanding items.

  5. Report to the community

    MPAC's oversight reports are published for public information.

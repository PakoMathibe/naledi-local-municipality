FREQUENTLY ASKED

Getting in touch.

What are the municipality's office hours?

Offices are open Monday to Friday, 07:30–16:00. The main switchboard on 053 928 2200 is available for urgent queries outside office hours.

Where are the municipal offices?

The main offices are at 19A Market Street, Vryburg, 8600. Stella and other service points are listed on the relevant service pages.

Will someone respond to my online enquiry?

This page is a demonstration — the form does not send or store information. On the production site, enquiries are routed to the correct department and acknowledged with a reference number.

How do I report a water or electricity fault?

Call 053 928 2200 for general faults, or 053 928 2252 for rapid response. For electrical issues, also see the Electricity page.

How do I submit a POPIA / access to information request?

PAIA (Promotion of Access to Information Act) requests are handled by the Information Officer. Contact the municipality on 053 928 2200 or by email to info@naledigov.co.za for the current form and process.

Where can I find tenders and vacancies?

See the Tenders and Vacancies pages for current listings. Both are updated as new opportunities are advertised.

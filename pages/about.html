ABOUT NALEDI

About the municipality.

Naledi Local Municipality is a Category B municipality situated in the western part of the Dr Ruth Segomotsi Mompati District Municipality, North West Province.

Municipal seat

Vryburg — the administrative and commercial centre of the municipality.

Communities served

Vryburg · Stella · Dithakwaneng · Devondale and surrounding farming communities.

Established

Current structure formed in 2000 under the Municipal Demarcation process.

Province & district

North West Province, within the Dr Ruth Segomotsi Mompati District Municipality.

WHO WE ARE

A municipality shaped by history.

Naledi Local Municipality takes its name from the Setswana word for "star", reflecting the municipality's ambition to shine as a model of service delivery in the North West Province. Our administrative centre, Vryburg, has a rich history dating from the late 1800s and today serves as the commercial hub of a vast agricultural region.

The municipality is one of five local municipalities in the Dr Ruth Segomotsi Mompati District, together with Kagisano-Molopo, Mamusa, Greater Taung and Naledi. Our work is guided by the constitutional mandate to provide basic, quality, sustainable and equitable services through effective governance and sound financial management.

Our communities are diverse — from the urban centre of Vryburg to farming communities around Stella and the rural settlements of Dithakwaneng and Devondale. We serve residents, businesses, farmers, investors, and visitors to the region.

OUR DIRECTION

Vision, mission and values.

Vision

A model municipality delivering quality, sustainable and equitable services that uplift every community within Naledi.

Mission

To provide affordable, reliable and accessible municipal services through accountable governance, community participation and sound financial management.

Values

Integrity · Accountability · Transparency · Service excellence · Batho Pele (People First) · Respect for diversity

COMMUNITIES SERVED

Four towns, one municipality.

Naledi Local Municipality serves communities across a wide geographic area in the western North West. Each has its own character and needs.

Vryburg

The municipal seat and largest town. A regional commercial and agricultural services centre on the N14, historically known as the "Athens of the North West" for its early educational heritage.

Stella

A farming town west of Vryburg, surrounded by cattle, game and maize farms. Home to a satellite municipal office serving the surrounding rural community.

Dithakwaneng

A rural settlement in the municipality's northern area, with strong community ties and ongoing focus on basic services, water access and housing.

Devondale

A small settlement serving the agricultural community, connected to the municipal road network and farming economy of the region.

LOCAL ECONOMY

What drives Naledi.

The Naledi economy is closely linked to agriculture — particularly cattle farming, game farming, maize and groundnuts — alongside mining, agri-processing and a growing services sector centred on Vryburg. The municipality works with the District, Province and private sector to grow local economic opportunities.

Agriculture

Cattle, game, maize, groundnuts and livestock production across the district.

Mining

Diamond and other mining activities in the wider district support local employment.

Services & retail

Vryburg serves as a commercial and retail hub for the surrounding farming region.

POWERS & FUNCTIONS

What we are responsible for.

In terms of Section 156 of the Constitution and the Municipal Systems Act, Naledi Local Municipality is responsible for a defined set of local functions.

Water and sanitation

Provision of potable water and sanitation services to households, businesses and institutions within the municipal area, including bulk supply arrangements with the district and province.

Electricity and energy

Distribution of electricity to residents within the licensed area, subject to the national electricity regulatory framework and coordination with Eskom.

Roads and stormwater

Construction and maintenance of municipal roads, sidewalks, street lighting and stormwater drainage networks in partnership with the province for regional roads.

Refuse removal and waste management

Household refuse collection, landfill management, illegal dumping response and waste minimisation in line with national environmental legislation.

Housing and human settlements

Facilitation of housing delivery in partnership with the provincial Department of Human Settlements, including the housing demand database and title deed programmes.

Municipal planning and development

Preparation of the Integrated Development Plan (IDP), land use management, town planning approvals and building control.

Local economic development

Support for small business, cooperatives, agriculture and tourism, in partnership with the district, province and private sector.

Public safety and disaster management

Fire and rescue services, disaster risk management, traffic and by-law enforcement within the municipal area.

Libraries, parks and recreation

Provision of municipal libraries, parks, sports fields and community facilities, in partnership with the province where applicable.

Property rates and revenue

Billing and collection of property rates and service charges, in line with the Municipal Property Rates Act and the Municipal Finance Management Act.

INTERGOVERNMENTAL RELATIONS

Working across spheres.

The Constitution establishes three spheres of government — national, provincial and local. Naledi works within this framework and with its district municipality.

District

Dr Ruth Segomotsi Mompati District Municipality — shared planning, bulk services and regional coordination.

Province

North West Provincial Government — housing delivery, roads, education, health and social development.

National

Departments including COGTA, Treasury, Water & Sanitation and Human Settlements.

OUR PILLARS

How we work.

  1. Service delivery

    Water, sanitation, electricity, refuse, roads and public safety for all serviced areas.

  2. Good governance

    Accountable council, transparent processes and public participation.

  3. Financial sustainability

    Sound budgeting, revenue collection and prudent expenditure.

  4. Community partnership

    Working with residents, businesses and other spheres of government.

  5. Institutional development

    Building skills, systems and technology to deliver better services.

FINANCE & REPORTS

Municipal budgets.

The approved MTREF budget, adjustment budgets, tariffs and related financial documents.

HOW THE BUDGET WORKS

A three-year plan.

The municipality adopts a Medium Term Revenue and Expenditure Framework (MTREF) budget each year, covering the budget year and the two following outer years. The budget is aligned with the IDP and the SDBIP, and approved by council before the start of the financial year on 1 July.

MTREF

Three-year budget framework aligned to the IDP.

Tariffs

Property rates and service tariffs for the year.

Adjustments

Adjustment budgets as required during the year.

Reporting

Monthly and quarterly budget statements.

THE BUDGET CYCLE

How the budget is adopted.

  1. Drafting (Aug – Feb)

    Departments prepare budget inputs in line with the IDP.

  2. Tabling (by 31 Mar)

    The Mayor tables the draft budget at council.

  3. Public comment (Apr – May)

    The draft budget is published for public comment in line with the MFMA.

  4. Adoption (by 30 Jun)

    Council adopts the final budget before the start of the financial year.

  5. Publication (Jul)

    The approved budget is published for public information.

  6. Adjustment budget (Jan – Feb)

    Adjustments tabled mid-year to account for changed circumstances.

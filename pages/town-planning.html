WHAT WE DO

Managing how land is used.

The Town Planning section is responsible for guiding how land in the municipality is used, ensuring that development is orderly, safe and aligned with the municipality's Spatial Development Framework and Land Use Management Scheme.

The section processes planning applications, provides planning advice, and enforces compliance with planning regulations, building standards and by-laws.

BUILDING PLANS

How to submit a building plan.

  1. Appoint a registered professional

    Building plans must be drawn and submitted by a registered professional (architect, draughtsperson or engineer).

  2. Prepare the submission

    Plans must comply with the National Building Regulations, the Land Use Management Scheme and local by-laws.

  3. Submit to Town Planning

    Submit the plans at the municipal offices at 19A Market Street, Vryburg.

  4. Pay the application fee

    A fee is payable on submission (fee schedule available on request).

  5. Await review

    Plans are reviewed against the applicable regulations and by-laws.

  6. Approval & inspections

    Once approved, construction may begin. Site inspections are conducted during construction.

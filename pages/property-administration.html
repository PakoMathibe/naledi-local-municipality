WHAT WE DO

Managing municipal land and property.

The Property Administration section is responsible for managing the municipality's land and property portfolio. This includes administering leases, facilitating the sale of land where applicable, maintaining property records, and supporting the municipality's property-related decisions.

The section works closely with Town Planning, Legal Services, Budget & Treasury, and the Office of the Municipal Manager to ensure municipal property is managed lawfully and in the public interest.

HOW TO APPLY

Applying for a lease.

  1. Identify the property

    Confirm the property you're interested in is municipal-owned and available for lease.

  2. Obtain the application form

    Request the lease application form from the Property Administration section.

  3. Prepare supporting documents

    ID, company registration (for businesses), financial information and any other documents required.

  4. Submit your application

    Submit at the municipal offices at 19A Market Street, Vryburg, with the application fee.

  5. Await review

    The application is assessed for compliance with the municipality's leasing policy.

  6. Lease signing

    If approved, a lease agreement is prepared and signed.

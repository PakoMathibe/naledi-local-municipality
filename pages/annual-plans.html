PLANNING & PERFORMANCE

Annual plans.

Each year the municipality translates the IDP into an annual plan and budget, then approves the SDBIP that becomes the operational plan. Below you'll find the current and archived annual plans for public download.

What is an annual plan?

An annual plan summarises the year's programmes, targets and budget allocations in line with the IDP. It is tabled with the draft budget for public comment, then adopted by council before the start of the financial year. Its implementation is tracked through the SDBIP and reported in the annual report.

Annual plan

Summarises the year's planned programmes against the IDP.

Budget

Approved MTREF budget aligned to the annual plan.

SDBIP

Implementation plan with KPIs and quarterly targets.

Public comment

Plans and budget are published for comment before adoption.

THE ANNUAL CYCLE

How annual plans are adopted.

  1. Drafting (Aug – Feb)

    Departments prepare the draft annual plan and budget in line with the IDP.

  2. Public comment (Mar – Apr)

    The draft is tabled and published for public comment in line with the MFMA.

  3. Council adoption (May – Jun)

    Council adopts the final annual plan and budget before 30 June.

  4. Publication (Jul)

    Approved plans and the budget are published for public information.

  5. SDBIP approval (Jul)

    The Mayor approves the SDBIP within 28 days of the budget.

LINKED TO THE IDP

Where the plan comes from.

The annual plan is built from the IDP's five-year priorities. Each service area in the plan maps back to an IDP priority. Read the IDP page for the strategic overview, or the SDBIP for the measurable targets.

Water & sanitation

Infrastructure, maintenance and access targets.

Electricity

Network upgrades and reliability improvements.

Housing

Settlement development and title deed programmes.

Roads & stormwater

Maintenance, rehabilitation and new construction.

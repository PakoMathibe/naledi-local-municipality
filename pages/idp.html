PLANNING & PERFORMANCE

Integrated Development Plan.

The IDP is the municipality's five-year strategic plan — developed with the community, aligned to the budget and delivered through the SDBIP.

WHAT IS THE IDP?

The municipality's master plan.

The Integrated Development Plan (IDP) is the strategic plan that guides all planning, budgeting and delivery in the municipality for a five-year council term. It reflects the priorities identified through public participation and sets out the municipality's development objectives.

Community priorities

Identified through consultation with residents and stakeholders.

Budget alignment

The IDP informs the annual budget and the SDBIP.

Measurable objectives

Priorities are translated into targets and performance indicators.

Public participation

Residents have a right to comment on and contribute to the IDP.

THE IDP CYCLE

How the IDP is developed.

  1. Preparation (Aug – Sep)

    Process plan adopted by council; consultation scheduled.

  2. Community consultation (Sep – Oct)

    Public meetings, ward inputs and written comments are gathered.

  3. Draft IDP (Nov – Mar)

    The draft is tabled and published for public comment.

  4. Public comment (Mar – Apr)

    The draft is open for public comment for at least 21 days.

  5. Adoption (May – Jun)

    Council adopts the final IDP and it guides the budget for the next financial year.

PRIORITY AREAS

What the IDP focuses on.

Naledi's IDP priorities typically cover these service areas — the current IDP will confirm the specific objectives for the term.

Water & sanitation

Water security, sanitation infrastructure and access for all.

Electricity

Network reliability, safety and electrification of unserved areas.

Roads & transport

Road maintenance, stormwater and public transport infrastructure.

Housing

Human settlements, title deeds and housing delivery.

Waste & environment

Waste management, landfill operations and environmental protection.

Local economic development

Business support, agriculture, tourism and job creation.

HOW TO GET INVOLVED

Have your say.

Attend public meetings

IDP roadshows and ward meetings are held during the consultation phase.

Submit written comments

Comments can be submitted to the municipality during the comment period.

Watch public notices

Comment deadlines and meeting dates are announced in the public notices.

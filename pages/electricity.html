WHAT WE PROVIDE

How electricity is supplied.

The municipality is licensed to distribute electricity in specific supply areas. Bulk electricity is purchased from Eskom and distributed to households, businesses and institutions through the municipal network. Certain areas — including some rural and peri-urban parts of the district — are supplied directly by Eskom.

Municipal supply areas

Vryburg, Stella and serviced areas. Billed through your municipal account.

Eskom supply areas

Some rural areas are supplied directly by Eskom. Contact Eskom on 0860 037 566.

Load shedding

Schedules are managed at national level. Check the Eskom portal or local notices for schedules.

HOW FAULT REPORTING WORKS

Reporting an outage.

1. Check the cause

Check if neighbours are also affected. If only your property is affected, check your main breaker.

2. Report to the municipality

Call 053 928 2290 or rapid response on 053 928 2252. Give your address and nearest intersection.

3. Follow up

Keep your reference number. Ask for updates if the outage is not resolved within expected timeframes.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Electricity questions.

My power is out — what should I do?

Check whether neighbours are also affected. If only your property is affected, check your main breaker. To report an area outage call 053 928 2290.

How do I apply for a new electricity connection?

Visit the Technical Services department at 19A Market Street, Vryburg, with your property details and a copy of your ID. A quotation will be issued for the connection.

How do I buy prepaid electricity?

Prepaid electricity can be purchased at authorised vendors and retail outlets. Keep your voucher until it has successfully loaded.

What do I do if I see a fallen power line?

Treat every line as live. Keep people and animals at least 10 metres away and call 053 928 2290 immediately.

Why is my prepaid meter not working?

Common causes include a flat meter battery, network connectivity or a meter fault. Report it to the electrical services team on 053 928 2290.

Who supplies my electricity — the municipality or Eskom?

Municipal supply areas are Vryburg, Stella and serviced parts of the district. Some rural areas are supplied directly by Eskom. If unsure, call 053 928 2200 with your address.

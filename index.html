ABOUT NALEDI

A municipality shaped by its people.

Naledi Local Municipality is situated in the western part of the Dr Ruth Segomotsi Mompati District Municipality in the North West Province.

Our work is guided by the responsibility to provide basic, quality, sustainable and equitable services through effective governance and financial management.

OUR VISIONQuality, sustainable and equitable services for our communities.
